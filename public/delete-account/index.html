Account & data

Delete your Nafs account

Use this page to request permanent deletion of your Nafs account and the personal data linked to it. You do not need to open the mobile app. Requests are handled by the Nafs support team on myva.ae.

How to request deletion

  1. Fill in the form below with the email address registered on your Nafs account.
  2. Submit the request. You will receive a confirmation email with a reference number.
  3. We verify ownership of the account (we may ask you to confirm from that email).
  4. After verification, we delete the account and associated personal data within 30 days (usually sooner). You will get a confirmation when it is done.

Prefer email? Write to support@myva.ae with the subject “Account deletion request”.

What is deleted

  • Account profile (name, email, phone, preferences, onboarding)
  • Mood check-ins, journals, goals, assessments, and My Plan history
  • Voice / photo / video recordings and other privacy media you uploaded
  • Chat messages, devices, notification preferences, and consents
  • Client bookings and related session notes tied only to your account

What may be retained, and for how long

  • Legal / financial records (e.g. invoices, payout records, fraud or dispute logs) may be kept up to 24 months where required by law or accounting rules, then deleted or anonymized.
  • Security logs (IP / access events) may be kept up to 90 days, then deleted.
  • Data that is already anonymized (no longer linked to you) may remain for product analytics.

Deletion is permanent. You will need to create a new account if you return to Nafs later.
